# Consent and ethics note (read before any survey)
* Obtain university ethics/faculty approval first. Do not contact any organisation under a false pretext; state you are a student project.
* Read this aloud: "This is a student research project on reducing food waste. Participation is voluntary, you may skip any question or stop at any
  time. We record business-level information only and will not publish your name without written permission."
* Record consent (Yes/No) and date. No personal data of beneficiaries. Store responses on a password-protected device; anonymise before sharing.
* Surveys ask what organisations **do and want**; they do not create any commitment. Never promise donations, pickups or publicity.
